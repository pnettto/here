# here

*A screen that breathes.*

**here** is a slow, endless journey of light for the screen in your home, a place to sit for a while and feel how astonishing it is to be alive.

It starts with a single light, breathing in the dark. Then you drift through the nebula where the atoms in you were made, watch the sun rise over the edge of the Earth from orbit, sink into sunlit water where life began, wake above a sea of clouds at dawn, and lie beneath the Milky Way beside a still lake. Then the breath begins again.

Everything breathes together at six breaths a minute, the rhythm of a calm body. Soft generative music plays alongside and never repeats the same way twice. Now and then a few words surface, slowly, one at a time: Sagan, Carson, Camus, Oliver, Feynman, and others who looked closely at the world and found it wondrous.

Nothing is a video. Every frame is generated live on your GPU, so no two moments are ever the same.

## Begin

Open `index.html` in a modern browser and click once. No install, no server.

Dim the room. Turn the sound up a little. Stay as long as you like.
